# Excitatory spinal dorsal-horn interneuron candidates for L796-ALT-PN

## Method

Queried NeuroMorpho directly through `/api/neuron/select`, merged records by `neuron_name`, fetched per-neuron metadata and morphometry where available, fetched PubMed/Crossref title/abstract metadata for cited papers, verified each retained NeuroMorpho page and SWC URL by HTTP 200, and validated downloaded SWCs for soma type-1 nodes, dendrite type-3 nodes, and >100 parsed points.

The score uses the requested 100-point decomposition: species 10, dorsal horn/lamina relevance 20, excitatory identity 20, pain relevance 20, morphology 15, SWC quality 10, electrophysiology 5. The requested passive/active-paper flag is reported separately and is not added to the score.
