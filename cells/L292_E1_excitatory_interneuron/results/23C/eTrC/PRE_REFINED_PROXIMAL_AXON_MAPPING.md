# Pre-refinement active diagnostics

The `broad_initial`, `extended_current_diagnostic`,
`rheobase_refinement_50pA`, and `rheobase_refinement_10pA` directories are
retained for provenance. They used a segment-centre definition of the
provisional 9-um proximal axonal initiation region without an additional
maximum segment-length constraint.

The geometry audit showed that one 249.25-um2 segment received the hillock
density, 5.88 times the released simplified hillock area. These runs are useful
diagnostics but are not accepted validation results. Subsequent active runs use
at most 3-um segments in sections intersecting the provisional 9-um region.
